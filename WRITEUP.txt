We came up with the idea of Adaptation by taking inspiration from Blooket. We liked how the game integrates questions into levelling up and upgrading, allowing you to learn while having fun, so we decided to make something similar to it, but for learning Python basics instead. We had multiple challenges, though the most impactful one was the extreme lack of time. We were given a very short timeframe to build the project, only 2 days, as we had to go to the core workshops. This meant we had to quickly plan features, decide which ideas to keep and to remove, code the game, and finish the slides and the write-up. To solve it, we split the work between all of the members of our group, allowing us to finish before the submission deadline.
